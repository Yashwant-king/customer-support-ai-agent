# Customer Verification Policy

## Purpose

Customer verification helps ensure that support actions are performed for the correct customer or order.

## Required Information

Depending on the request, the support system may require information such as:

- Order ID
- Customer information associated with the order
- Relevant product information

## Order Verification

Before providing order-specific information or performing an order-related action, the support system should verify that the order exists.

The system should use the customer and order information available in the company database.

## Sensitive Information

The support agent must not expose unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Only information required to resolve the customer's request should be provided.

## Important Rules

The agent must not:

- Guess customer information.
- Guess an Order ID.
- Provide another customer's order information.
- Perform an action when the required information cannot be verified.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Customer identity or order ownership cannot be adequately verified.
- The customer disputes the verification result.
- The request involves sensitive information that requires human review.
