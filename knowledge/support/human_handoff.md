# Human Support Handoff Policy

## Human Handoff

Customers may request assistance from a human support agent.

The automated support system should respect a clear request for human assistance.

## When Handoff Is Required

A human handoff should occur when:

- The customer explicitly requests a human agent.
- The case requires a policy exception.
- The automated system cannot determine a reliable answer.
- The customer disputes an automated decision.
- The issue involves conflicting or incomplete information.
- The issue requires authorization that the automated system does not have.

## Information to Preserve

When handing a case to a human agent, preserve relevant:

- Customer information
- Order ID
- Order information
- Payment information when relevant
- Conversation history
- Reason for escalation

## Important Rules

The automated agent must not:

- Pretend that a human agent has already reviewed the case when this has not happened.
- Claim that an escalation was completed without actually performing the handoff.
- Invent a human agent response.

## Customer Communication

The customer should be clearly informed when the case is being transferred or escalated to human support.
