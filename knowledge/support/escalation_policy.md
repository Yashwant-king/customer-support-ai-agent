# Support Escalation Policy

## When to Escalate

A support case should be escalated to a human agent when the automated support system cannot safely or confidently resolve the request.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalation is appropriate when:

- Required information is missing.
- Database information is inconsistent.
- The customer disputes a decision.
- The customer requests a policy exception.
- A refund or replacement decision requires human authorization.
- The customer reports a problem that cannot be resolved using available policies.
- The customer explicitly requests human assistance.

## Uncertainty

The support agent must not guess when information is unavailable or ambiguous.

When the system cannot determine the correct answer, it should explain the limitation and escalate the case when appropriate.

## Policy Exceptions

The automated agent must not independently approve exceptions to company policies.

Policy exceptions require human review.

## Communication

When escalating, the customer should be told that the case requires human assistance.

The agent should preserve relevant conversation and order information for the human support agent.
