# Damaged Package Policy

## Damaged Delivery

Customers who receive a package containing a damaged or defective product should report the problem to support.

The support agent should verify the order before determining the appropriate resolution.

## Required Information

The customer should provide:

- Order ID
- Product involved
- Description of the damage
- Photos or other evidence when requested

## Resolution

Depending on the circumstances, the customer may be eligible for:

- Product replacement
- Refund
- Another applicable resolution

The replacement and refund policies determine the applicable process.

## Important Rules

The support agent must not:

- Promise a replacement without checking eligibility.
- Promise a refund without checking the applicable policy.
- Promise a specific replacement delivery date without supporting information.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The customer disputes the proposed resolution.
- The requested replacement is unavailable.
- The customer requests an exception.
- There is insufficient information to determine the appropriate resolution.
