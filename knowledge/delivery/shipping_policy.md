# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Information

Customers can ask about the shipping and delivery of their orders.

The support system should use the order database to determine the current order status and available delivery information.

## Estimated Delivery

An estimated delivery date is not a guaranteed delivery date.

The support agent must clearly distinguish between an estimated delivery date and a confirmed delivery date.

## Shipping Status

The support agent may provide the customer's current shipping status when that information is available in the order database.

The agent must not invent:

- Shipping status
- Carrier information
- Tracking information
- Delivery dates

## Delivery Information

When available, the agent may provide:

- Estimated delivery date
- Shipment status
- Confirmed delivery date

## Missing Information

If the available order information does not contain enough information to answer the customer's question, the agent should explain the limitation rather than guessing.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human support agent when:

- Shipping information is inconsistent.
- The customer disputes the available information.
- The order requires investigation beyond the available database information.
