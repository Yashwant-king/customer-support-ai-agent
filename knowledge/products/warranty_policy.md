# Product Warranty Policy

## Warranty

Some products may be covered by a manufacturer or company warranty.

Warranty eligibility depends on the specific product and applicable warranty terms.

## Warranty Request

The customer should provide:

- Order ID
- Product involved
- Description of the problem

Additional information or evidence may be requested.

## Verification

The support agent must verify the product and order information before confirming warranty eligibility.

The agent must not assume that every product is covered by a warranty.

## Warranty Resolution

Depending on the applicable warranty terms, a customer may receive an appropriate resolution such as:

- Repair
- Replacement
- Other applicable support

The agent must follow the applicable warranty terms.

## Important Rules

The support agent must not:

- Promise warranty coverage without verification.
- Promise a replacement without checking eligibility.
- Invent warranty conditions.
- Make unauthorized exceptions.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Warranty eligibility is unclear.
- The customer disputes the warranty decision.
- The customer requests an exception.
- Available product information is insufficient.
