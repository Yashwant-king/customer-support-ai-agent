# Product Replacement Policy

## Damaged Products

Customers who receive a product that is damaged, defective, or materially different from the product ordered may request a replacement.

A replacement request should normally be submitted within 7 days of delivery.

## Required Information

The customer should provide:

- Order ID
- Description of the damage or defect
- Product involved
- Photos or other evidence when requested

## Replacement Availability

A replacement is subject to product availability.

If the same product is unavailable, the customer may be offered:

1. A refund, or
2. An alternative product when applicable.

## Delivery

Replacement delivery time depends on product availability and shipping options.

The support agent must not promise a specific delivery date unless the available order/shipping information confirms that date.

## Refund Instead of Replacement

If a replacement cannot be provided, the customer may request a refund according to the refund policy.

## Escalation

Escalate the case to a human support agent when:

- The customer disputes the replacement decision.
- The requested replacement is unavailable but the customer rejects the available alternatives.
- The customer requests an exception to the policy.
- There is insufficient information to make a decision.
