# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

A customer may be eligible for a refund when an order is successfully cancelled, a returned product qualifies for a refund, or another applicable company policy provides a refund.

The support agent must verify the relevant order and payment information before confirming a refund.

## Refund Amount

The refund amount depends on the applicable transaction and refund decision.

The support agent must use available payment information rather than estimating or inventing a refund amount.

## Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, processing depends on the payment method and the applicable payment process.

The support agent must not promise an exact refund date unless the available information confirms it.

## Payment Verification

Before discussing a payment-related refund, the support system should check the available payment information for the order.

## Important Rules

The support agent must not:

- Claim that a refund has been processed without evidence.
- Invent a refund amount.
- Promise an exact refund date without supporting information.
- Approve policy exceptions without authorization.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Payment information conflicts with the order information.
- The customer disputes the refund amount.
- The customer disputes refund eligibility.
- The customer requests a policy exception.
- Available information is insufficient.
