# Payment Methods Policy

## Payment Information

Customers may ask about the payment method used for an order.

The support system should use the payment information stored in the company database.

## Supported Payment Information

The system may contain information such as:

- Payment type
- Payment amount
- Number of installments
- Payment sequence

The support agent must only provide information that is available in the database.

## Payment Privacy

The support agent must not expose sensitive payment credentials or confidential financial information.

Only the payment information necessary to resolve the customer's request should be provided.

## Multiple Payments

An order may contain multiple payment records.

The support system should consider all relevant payment records when determining the payment information for an order.

## Important Rules

The support agent must not:

- Invent a payment method.
- Invent a payment amount.
- Claim a payment succeeded without database evidence.
- Expose sensitive payment credentials.
