# Refund Processing Policy

## Refund Processing

A refund may be initiated when the applicable order, return, cancellation, or replacement policy allows it.

The support agent must verify the relevant order and payment information.

## Refund Status

The agent should distinguish between:

- Refund eligible
- Refund requested
- Refund processed
- Refund completed

These statuses must not be treated as equivalent.

## Processing Time

Refund processing time can depend on the payment method and payment provider.

The support agent must not promise an exact refund completion date unless confirmed by available information.

## Refund Amount

The refund amount should be based on the applicable transaction and approved refund.

The agent must not invent or estimate the amount.

## Missing Information

If the system does not contain enough information to determine the refund status, the agent should explain the limitation and escalate when necessary.

## Important Rules

The support agent must not:

- Claim that a refund was processed without evidence.
- Claim that a refund was completed when it is only requested.
- Promise an unsupported completion date.
- Invent a refund amount.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The customer reports a missing refund.
- Payment information conflicts with the refund information.
- The customer disputes the refund amount.
- The available information is insufficient.
