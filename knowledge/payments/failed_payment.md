# Failed Payment Policy

## Failed Payments

A payment may fail because of problems during the payment process.

The support agent should verify the available payment information before explaining the situation to the customer.

## Required Information

The customer should provide:

- Order ID
- Description of the payment problem

## Verification

The support system should check the payment records associated with the order.

The agent must not assume that a payment failed or succeeded without supporting information.

## Order and Payment Status

An order status and payment record may provide different pieces of information.

If the available information is inconsistent, the case should be investigated rather than guessed.

## Resolution

Depending on the situation, the customer may need to:

- Retry the payment
- Use another available payment method
- Wait for a payment issue to resolve
- Contact a human support agent

The agent must not invent a specific reason for a failed payment when the database does not provide one.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Payment information is inconsistent.
- The customer believes money was charged despite a failed order.
- The customer disputes the payment status.
- Available information is insufficient.
